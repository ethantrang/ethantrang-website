fear of failure is a silent killer. especially after achieving a certain level of "success" and pressure to sustain and grow it. 

seems to strip away motivation, optimism, delusion, when those things got you wins the first time. 

it might come from ego, to not appear weak, to always appear evergreen and knowing what you're doing. 

it's crazy how much i think it can hold you back
