i believe life is deterministic. 

an example i find crazy is that a person can move their muscles in their throat, generate a flow of air that becomes sounds, pushing the particles of air between them and another person, creating waves that vibrate another person's eardrums, whose brain then converts those seemingly meaningless vibrations into understanding and insight, and go on to change the course of their life with that information.

i've had experiences of when people have said things to me that i've applied and have significantly changed my position. 

my personal mental model for a deterministic life is pixel theory. 

you know how screens are just comprised of a grid of pixels flashing red, green, or blue. transform that 2d grid into an infinite 3d grid one (representing the space of our world) and replace pixels with atoms (or any singular unit of matter).

imagine life is just a 3d simulation of pixels flashing, maybe in some form of causation to each other, but it doesn't matter that much because in the end life is just a series of temporary occupied "colors" in space at any give time. that each individual snapshot of life, stripped down to pure information about the position of pixels and what "color" they are, seems almost completely trivial. 

in some ways this thought could be a nihilistic view and that nothing matters so why bother. but somehow it's fun to see how life plays out, how the plot of the movie unfolds. 

while i do believe my life up until this point has just been a sequence of causal events or a predetermined encoding. i am a believer in optimism of my life, and trust that it will be an exciting one.
