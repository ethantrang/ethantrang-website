i'm turning 21 tomorrow. 

it's that time that gets you thinking about the what's changed in the past year. 

it honestly felt like a long time, filled with some crazy highs and lows. had some first big wins with my career, but also struggled through some hard times of doubt, anxiety, and stress. 

12 weeks ago was one of those hard times. where i didn't know if I should keep going or not down this route. I've learned that I probably suffered more in imagination than reality. 

It got me thinking, when people tell their stories of said hard times, the times when they were in the trenches, was it as bad as it seemed. I know for myself can probably justify how relatively not bad my situation was. Wondering if the hard I'm going through is actually "tough tough" enough so that it will success down the line. Who knows, but I guess personal judgment on that is important, that you find yourself pushing yourself to limits you never knew possible. I think it's the trait that matters.
